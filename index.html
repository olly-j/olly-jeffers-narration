01 · About

A return to
the real deal.

I trained as an actor and singer before taking an unexpected detour into corporate learning and leadership development.

It turned out to be a useful detour. I have spent much of my career communicating ideas, working with audiences and finding the human story inside subjects that can otherwise feel complicated or remote.

Alongside that career, I continued voice-over and stage work. Now I’m returning seriously to narration—bringing together the performance training I started with and everything I have learned since about clarity, character and holding an audience.

I begin with the writing: what the author means, where the emotional weight sits, how the argument develops and what should remain quietly understated.
